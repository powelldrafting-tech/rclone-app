Privacy policy

This app is used only by its owner, to sync their own files between their own computer and their own Google Drive.

  • It accesses Google Drive only to copy the owner's files to and from their computer.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, shared, or sold. Files move only between the owner's computer and Google Drive.
  • The Google sign-in is stored only on the owner's computer and can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
